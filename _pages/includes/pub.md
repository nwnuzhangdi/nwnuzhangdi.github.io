AMS-SemiCD: A Synergistic Framework for Semi-Supervised Change Detection via an Adaptive Pseudo-Labeling Strategy and a Multi-Branch Network 

**Di Zhang**, Peicheng Yue , Huifang Ma, Zhanjun Hao, Xin He, Yun Liu, Jiaqi Zhao

**IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing( Volume: 64)** 

<span class="journal-tag">中科院1区TOP CCF-B</span>  [**[Paper]**](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11693064) <strong><span class='show_paper_citations' data='4FA6C0AAAAAJ:qjMakFHDy7sC'></span></strong>  [**[Code]**](https://github.com/RSII-NWNU/AMS-SemiCD) <strong><span class='show_paper_citations' data='4FA6C0AAAAAJ:qjMakFHDy7sC'></span></strong>

- We introduce AMS-SemiCD, a synergistic semi-supervised change detection framework based on Teacher–Student consistency regularization, combining MSE-Net with CNN–Mamba branches for local–global bitemporal feature learning and ASPR for class-adaptive pseudo-label refinement, thereby achieving 91.17% F1 and 83.78% IoU on LEVIR-CD with only 20% labeled data.
